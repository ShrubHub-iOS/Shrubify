# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta3

- Now Playing blends into the native iPad header and keeps Spotify's pane and menu actions. In landscape, artwork and playback controls are centered as a group. In split view, artwork scales to leave room for playback, volume and shortcuts.
- The iPad dock also recognizes Spotify's compact tab wrapper. A single system glass effect sits over page content. The dock clears known native backings and bounds the bar's visual output to the capsule without replacing the tab actions.
- The mini-player can mount glass through Spotify's own bar controller when the outer wrapper does not expose its view. Glass settings show the dimensions of an attached effect.
- This prerelease carries forward beta 2's lyrics fallback. The stable channel remains on 4.2.4.
