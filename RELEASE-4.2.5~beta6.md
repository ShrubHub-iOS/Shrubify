# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta6

- The iPad dock keeps its compact layout through playback transitions. Glass stays behind Spotify's controls, and the capsule no longer masks the tab buttons.
- The split-view mini-player can attach system glass when Spotify hosts it in a full-height wrapper. The bar's artwork color is kept off the glass when Spotify repaints it.
- Now Playing can use Spotify's loaded cover while higher-resolution artwork arrives. The player keeps its backdrop in place during layout changes and preserves the native header controls.
- The current lyric stays visible between closely spaced lines. Word highlight timings still follow the provider, and the preview clears during longer breaks.
