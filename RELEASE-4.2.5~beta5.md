# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta5

- iPad Now Playing uses larger adaptive track typography, a lyric line aligned under the cover, and a lighter playback timeline. The lyric highlights the current timed fragment when the provider supplies word timing.
- The player preserves the native header chrome and fades into the artwork below it. It restores Spotify's original cover drawing when leaving the player or opening its artwork viewer.
- lrc.red and LyricsPlus are available in Lyrics settings and Automatic Selection. lrc.red uses its native TTML. LyricsPlus preserves the word and line timing supplied by its V2 API. Source preferences retain their existing order.
- The previous dock design carries forward. iPad screenshots and the two new provider routes are covered in the beta testing checklist.
