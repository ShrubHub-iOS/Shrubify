What makes Shrubify different

More than just music playback.

Shrubify adds useful ways to make your listening experience feel more personal, without turning the familiar interface into something completely unrecognizable.

01

Shrubify Studio

Customize the Now Playing interface with different layouts, artwork styling, player elements, and saved presets. Make your player feel like yours.

02

Shrubify Replay

Explore locally recorded listening statistics, including your favorite artists, frequently played tracks, listening history, and recent activity.

03

ShrubPanel

Access Shrubify features from one convenient place. Jump between player settings, listening statistics, customization tools, and more.

04

ShrubQueue

Keep useful listening shortcuts close by, bookmark tracks, and save listening snapshots for later reference.

05

Liquid Glass

Enjoy supported native iOS interface effects with a clean appearance. Shrubify preserves Spotify's familiar color scheme instead of forcing a new theme.

06

Personal Controls

Fine-tune the interface with customization options, cleaner layouts, and stability settings designed to help with compatibility.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

A few useful things to know before downloading Shrubify.

What exactly is Shrubify?
Shrubify is an unofficial community-developed modification of the Spotify iOS experience. It builds on existing open-source tweak work and adds its own customization tools, interface options, and listening features.
Is Shrubify officially associated with Spotify?
No. Shrubify is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Spotify. Spotify and its trademarks belong to their respective owners.
Does Shrubify change Spotify's colors?
Shrubify aims to preserve the familiar Spotify appearance by default. Optional player customizations and native interface effects let you adjust the experience without requiring a completely different color scheme.
What is Shrubify Studio?
Shrubify Studio provides controls for supported player appearance settings, with layout presets such as Classic, Minimal, and Immersive. Available options can depend on the installed release and Spotify version.
Does Shrubify Replay track my listening?
Replay provides optional, locally stored listening statistics. Tracking is off until enabled. The resulting statistics are estimates based on recorded activity rather than a complete Spotify account history.
How do I get updates?
New releases can be found through the Shrubify repository on ShrubLibrary. You can also check GitHub Releases for build details.
Where can I report a bug?
Visit the Shrubify issue tracker. Include your iOS version, Spotify version, Shrubify release, and a description of the problem to help with troubleshooting.
