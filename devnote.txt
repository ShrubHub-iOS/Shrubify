Shrubify — Made by shrubbery & the ShrubHub team.

A cleaner, independent interface and compatibility controls built on established open-source foundations. Spotify colors are left unchanged unless you customize them in Appearance.

Found a crash? Try Stability Mode in Shrubify diagnostics, restart, and export the debug log when reporting it.
