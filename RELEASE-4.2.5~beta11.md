- Album artwork opens Spotify's tilt viewer when available. Shrubify's viewer adds a soft reflection that follows touch and device tilt, with support for Reduce Motion.
- The artist card shows the full photo within a compact area.
- Both artist links return the fullscreen player to the sidebar and open the artist page inside Spotify.
- The down arrow minimizes playback to the bottom-right mini-player. The fullscreen button switches between the sidebar and fullscreen.
