# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta4

- The iPad player reserves space for the live lyric directly below the cover. Tap the line to open full lyrics. Playback updates separately from layout, and the header blend ends before the native cover.
- Album covers prefer the large artwork variants where Spotify supplies them. Tapping a cover opens Spotify's tilt viewer when the cover exposes its native action, or an interactive artwork detail otherwise.
- System glass starts neutral. Explicit accent colors apply only in Tinted mode, and zero tint clears them. Dock geometry retries after transient pane changes, and the mini-player clears backing inside its known host.
- Auto-selected lyrics can display a timed result while the search continues for verified word timing. The lyrics view identifies line, partial word, and word timing, and preserves text that has no individual timestamps.
- The iPad simulator covers production-control interactions and glass rendering. Check the native artwork presentation, compact bars and split transitions on the physical iPad.
