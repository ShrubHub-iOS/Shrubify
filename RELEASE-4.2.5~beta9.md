# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta9

- Expand and exit fullscreen from the Shrubify player. Scroll down for the built-in About the artist and Song credits sections.

- The cover remains more visible through the softened, darkened backdrop. Live word timing and the under-cover lyric presentation carry forward.
- The dock aligns the selection under the drawn tab and skips an unmeasurable highlight during transitions.
