# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta7

- The under-cover lyric now crossfades between lines and eases its word highlight in sync with playback. The lyric area stays in place and honors Reduce Motion.
- The tab capsule stays centered when Spotify temporarily hides a tab. The selection follows Spotify's reported state. The mini-player uses Regular glass beneath its controls for contrast over busy pages.
- The split player can reuse verified artwork from the mini-player and other tracks on the same album. Placeholders are rejected, and a higher-resolution cover can replace the thumbnail.
- The fullscreen backdrop becomes opaque at the header boundary, covering the second artwork layer during transitions.
