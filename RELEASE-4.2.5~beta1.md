# Shrubify 4.2.5~beta1 — iPad glass and lyrics

4.2.5 beta 1 is for testing the iPad dock, mini-player and Now Playing in full screen and split view.

- The dock measures its controls after the compact layout is applied. The dock and mini-player share ownership of the native backing when they sit in the same wrapper. Each uses one iPadOS 26 system glass effect.
- Bright Canvas frames get more shading where the player puts text and controls. The full-screen player retains native iPad playback.
- The player lyrics button opens immediately and offers a retry. A lookup for another track cannot cancel a lookup for the playing song, and a failed custom lookup can use Spotify's original response.
- Includes fixes for lyric timing settings, scrubbing, line endings, saved queue navigation and Replay bookmarks.

On iPad, scroll Home and an album, switch tabs, open and close the split player, then play “Conversations” and “Bad Energy” and open Lyrics. Use the [device checklist](TESTING.md) to check colors, selection, playback and timing. The beta is distributed separately from the stable AltStore feed.
